# Findings memo - are the calls discrete or graded once recording confounds are controlled?

**Answer.** Graded, with one separable exception - not two discrete regimes. The draft's two clusters are a split between short, mostly silent recordings and longer, voiced-rich ones. On voiced frames and on single syllables, fuzzy c-means collapses to one cluster, nothing is stable across bats, and syllable duration is a smooth unimodal continuum. The exception is a group of pups' Isolation-context calls (6-7% of files): it is found blind by UMAP + HDBSCAN, separates from all other calls on unseen bats (AUC 0.98) and survives holding out channels, treatments and sessions. The draft's emitter and context labels were also attached to different recordings than the audio that was clustered, so its association claims are not usable as published.

**What was done.** 41,116 annotated files (Prat et al. 2017; 12 channels, 30 archives, 42 emitter groups = 41 bats + "unknown") were read from remote archives; no audio was kept. Features: whole span (the draft's method), voiced frames only (the span is ~87% unvoiced) and per syllable (141,815). Bats - never calls - were resampled. Also run: Dataset B, AVES embeddings, a VAE, a power analysis, 15 clustering algorithms on 19 representations, and out-of-sample replication on the 45,557 annotated files not used before (12,000 random + all 33,557 remaining), so every one of the 87,986 annotated files has been analysed once; the 205,252 unannotated recordings were not used. Every choice and changed number is in `logs/analysis_log.md`; the paper draft is `paper/main.tex`.

## Findings
1. **The draft reproduces exactly once its file numbers are read correctly.** They are row indices of `Annotations.csv`, not FileIDs (June emitter label matches row n in 100.0% of rows, the FileID in 0.1%). Then PCA correlation |r| = 1.000, FPC 0.5786, sizes 1,860/2,037 and the paper's duration contrast (1.966 vs 1.314 s) are reproduced.
2. **The July emitter/context labels belong to other files** (agree with the audio's true labels in 0.2% / 22.6% of rows). With true labels cluster x context V = 0.311 (bias-corrected 0.307, bat-level 95% CI 0.15-0.43; published 0.145); within channel x month strata 0.299 vs null 0.235.
3. **The draft pipeline clusters silence and duration**: whole-span label vs silence fraction AUC 0.887 (0.856-0.906), voiced duration 0.905 inverted, SNR 0.477; recording variables alone predict it on unseen bats with AUC 0.968.
4. **Voiced frames: FPC at c = 2 is exactly 0.500**, the one-cluster value; single-population simulations also give 0.500, so "all indices pick c = 2" is uninformative. HDBSCAN returns one dominant mass plus micro-clusters, never a halving.
5. **Tests that allow k = 1** (bats resampled; copula and session-mean nulls): prediction strength 0.66 (0.60-0.85) vs 0.8 rule, bootstrap Jaccard 0.84 vs 0.99/0.96, split-sample dip median p 0.99; gap statistic uninformative.
6. **Power** (mixtures spiked into a null): the battery detects two equal-share types at >= 4 SD separation (90-100% with 200 replicates per cell and Wilson intervals; 80-92% for syllable shapes), a 20% minority at >= 6 SD, a 5% minority only at 8 SD (dip statistic). A negative global result excludes well-separated common types, not rare or overlapping ones.
7. **The exception - pups' Isolation calls.** UMAP + HDBSCAN on 12,000 voiced files returns 2 clusters; the small one (6.4% of files) is 86% Isolation-context, holds 83% of Isolation files and is stable across min size, UMAP neighbours and bat bootstraps (Jaccard 0.975); among pups 88% of Isolation files vs 2% of the same pups' other calls. HDBSCAN counts and noise shares are fragile (the real data minus the cluster fragments too), so the evidence is supervised: Isolation vs rest on unseen bats AUC 0.984 (0.979-0.991) with a bimodal held-out score (non-Isolation scores alone unimodal); pups only 0.982; within channel x month strata 0.90; leave-one-channel-out 0.983, leave-one-treatment-out 0.986; per-pup median 0.97 (15 pups); 0.984 without the unknown-emitter group (57% of Isolation files). Syllables: AUC 0.956, with an overlapping tail. UMAP also recovers it from the draft's whole-span features (91% pure, next to channel-specific clusters) and from syllable shape, not from voiced MFCC statistics.
8. **Replicated out of sample, twice** (criteria C1 AUC >= 0.90, C2 a 3-12% cluster >= 70% Isolation, C3 nulls fragment more, written to the log before the files were analysed). 12,000 random never-used files: AUC 0.988 (0.978-0.992); fresh UMAP + HDBSCAN gives a 5.8% cluster that is 90% Isolation-context. All 33,557 remaining annotated files (2,089 Isolation): AUC 0.991 (0.983-0.995), d' 5.1, pups only 0.989; a 5.6% cluster of 673 files from 26 emitter groups, 91.5% Isolation, holding 82% of the Isolation files of the 12,000-file UMAP subsample; the copula nulls give 7-8 clusters with 64-74% noise. The negative results replicate in both (voiced FPC 0.5000; prediction strength 0.70 / 0.66 vs null 0.95 / 0.955; whole-span label vs silence AUC 0.90 / 0.90).
9. **Syllables.** Median 85 ms; log-duration unimodal (dip p = 0.18). Duration-free shape carries context information on unseen bats (balanced accuracy 0.198; chance 0.077) that duration does not (0.066). A VAE trained on half the bats and AVES (2 models x 4 variants, 1,000 files; FCM collapses 8/8) add no structure or context information; AVES separates Isolation no better than handcrafted (AUC 0.93-0.95 vs 0.94-0.97).
10. **Identity is weakly transferable across microphones.** 40 bats: random 0.593, day-blocked 0.560, channel-blocked 0.202 (balanced 0.125 vs within-stratum permutation null 0.036; chance 0.025); per-channel normalisation does not rescue it (0.083-0.099). Within one channel on unseen days 0.648 vs 0.326 majority. Pair-coded files (16,663): 0.461 vs null 0.290. Voice and roost position cannot be separated here.
11. **Mixed models.** Context stays significant with random effects for bat and channel x month (LR chi2 1,485, voiced); repeatability by bat 0.13 (0.10-0.19).
12. **Dataset B** mostly agrees (mic clusters follow duration, AUC 0.89); event length is multimodal (dip p < 1e-4) but events are cut by the authors' detector, so its origin is unresolved.
13. **The June table**: 16,000 "balanced" calls are 9,588 distinct rows; its cluster is the same silence/length split; its two "specialist" bats share treatment 9 and long files. Review: `results/REVIEW_of_june_draft.md`.
14. **Every clustering algorithm on every representation** (15 algorithms, 26 variants, 19 representations, 1,558 configurations, 0 errors; 6,000 points each). On the four file-level Dataset-A representations no algorithm gave a balanced (larger cluster <= 80%), well-separated (silhouette >= 0.25) and bat-stable (transfer ARI >= 0.6) partition. Well-separated, stable partitions do exist, but only as a ~6% minority that is the Isolation group (spectral on voiced features: silhouette 0.33, ARI 0.91, Isolation F1 0.87). Isolation was recovered (precision >= 0.7 and recall >= 0.5) by spectral (13 of 17 representations in some configuration), Leiden, Louvain, SOM, Ward, BIRCH and tied GMM, by k-means in 7, never by fuzzy c-means or by DBSCAN / OPTICS / mean-shift / affinity propagation. So fuzzy c-means, the draft's method, never recovered the one real group, although several other methods did; "the answer depends on the algorithm" holds for that group and not for a balanced two-regime split.
15. **Sensitivity**: with standardised features the FCM collapse holds for 20-100 PCs; with 5-10 PCs or unscaled features FCM does not collapse but splits on silence/duration again (AUC 0.72-0.81). Prediction strength stays below 0.8 and below the copula null in every setting.

## Limits
Only 41 bats have positive emitter codes (48% of annotations are pair-coded, 9% unknown). Pup age is confounded with Isolation (pups < 40 days give almost only Isolation files; within age windows from day 40 the AUC is 0.70-0.97). Isolation is also a recording situation: the group is "separable and robust to bat, session, channel and treatment", not proven to be a call type without playback or within-situation contrasts. Many analyses were exploratory; the UMAP finding emerged post hoc (supervised tests and pre-specified replications on 12,000 and 33,557 unseen files confirm it, but within the same corpus and colony). Unsupervised recovery depends on representation. The dip test has little power for minorities < 10%. The original extraction code is missing, so the cause of the label shift is unknown. Dataset B has no context labels; the AVES comparison is small (1,000 files).
